# Privacy Policy — Gliding Compman

**Last updated: April 10, 2026**

## Overview

Gliding Compman ("the app") is a mobile application for browsing gliding competitions and downloading flight files for use with XCSoar. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data We Do Not Collect

The app does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

- No account registration or login is required.
- No names, email addresses, or contact details are collected.
- No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data are collected.
- No location data is accessed or recorded.
- The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Your list of saved/bookmarked competitions.
- Competition data (names, dates, classes) cached from SoaringSpot for offline viewing.
- Flight task, airspace, and waypoint files downloaded at your explicit request, saved to your XCSoar data folder.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

The app fetches publicly available competition information from **SoaringSpot** ([soaringspot.com](https://www.soaringspot.com)). When you browse competitions or download files, your device makes network requests to SoaringSpot servers. SoaringSpot's own privacy policy governs any data they collect from those requests.

No other third-party services, APIs, or cloud backends are used.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. As the app collects no personal data, significant changes are unlikely.
